## 🌍 The MindSettler Impact

MindSettler represents a shift from **Reactive Care** to **Proactive Understanding**. 

- **Technically**: It's a decoupled, RAG-powered intelligence system.
- **Socially**: It's a low-barrier, stigma-free bridge to clinical support.
- **Business-wise**: It's a scalable knowledge platform with a growing "Institutional Memory" moat.
