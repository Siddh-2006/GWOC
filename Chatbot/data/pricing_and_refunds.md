# Pricing and Refunds

## Pricing
Pricing for sessions is dynamic and may vary based on the therapist, session duration, and mode (Online vs. Offline).
*   **To Check Prices**: Please start the booking process. You will see the exact price for each available slot before you confirm.

## Refund Policy
*   **Processing Time**: Refunds are initiated within **24 hours** of cancellation validation.
*   **Conditions**: Please refer to the cancellation terms in the booking flow. Generally, notice is required.

## Payments
*   Links are sent via email.
*   Payments must be completed to confirm Online slots.
